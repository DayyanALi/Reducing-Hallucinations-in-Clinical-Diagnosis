### A - Assessment

1. **Migraine Headache:** The presentation of a unilateral, throbbing headache with visual disturbances, nausea, vomiting, photophobia, and a similar episode recently suggests a migraine. The patient is worried about brain cancer due to a recent personal loss and internet searches, but clinical features do not support this differential.

### P - Plan

1. **Migraine Headache:**
   - **Actions:**
     - Advise keeping a headache diary to identify triggers.
     - Educate on potential triggers and management strategies.
   - **Medications:**
     - Suggest trial of Paracetamol, Co-codamol, or NSAIDs such as Ibuprofen or Naproxen (dose/route/frequency not documented).
   - **Monitoring:**
     - Observe headache frequency and severity.
   - **Follow-up:** Discuss prophylactic treatments if migraines persist, and consider further consultation next week.
   - **Patient Instructions:** 
     - Return if headaches worsen, do not respond to medications, or if there are new symptoms like neck pain, limb weakness, or changes in vision.
   - **Disposition:** Outpatient management with close monitoring.
