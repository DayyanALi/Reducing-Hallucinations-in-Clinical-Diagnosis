## Assessment

1. **Suspected anaphylactic reaction**  
   Patient presents with progressive lip swelling, difficulty breathing, and dizziness following ingestion of prawn soup, consistent with an acute allergic reaction possibly progressing to anaphylaxis. There is a history of a previous severe allergic reaction.  
   - Differential: Allergic angioedema, anaphylaxis, other causes of lip swelling (less likely given clinical context).

2. **Asthma**  
   Known asthma requiring salbutamol inhaler use. Respiratory symptoms may be exacerbated by allergic reaction.

## Plan

**For Suspected Anaphylaxis:**  
- Immediate call for emergency medical services (ambulance) for urgent evaluation, possible administration of epinephrine injection, and further management.  
- Advise administration of available oral antihistamines while awaiting ambulance.  
- Monitor respiratory status and consciousness while waiting for EMS.  
- Patient education on avoidance of known allergens and importance of carrying emergency epinephrine auto-injectors in the future.  
- Recommend follow-up after acute episode for allergy workup and possible prescription of epinephrine auto-injector.

**For Asthma:**  
- Continue use of salbutamol inhaler as used previously.  
- Monitor respiratory symptoms closely in context of allergic reaction.

**Disposition:**  
- Ambulance called for transport to hospital for acute care.

**Follow-up:**  
- Patient advised to contact primary care or allergy specialist post-discharge for further management and secondary prevention.
