## A - Assessment

**1. Acute gastroenteritis**  
Likely viral or infectious gastroenteritis characterized by diarrhea and vomiting resolving over three days, with symptoms of dehydration and weakness. No fever at present. No blood in stools or abdominal pain, reducing concern for invasive or complicated infectious process.

**Differential diagnosis:**  
- Infectious gastroenteritis (viral, bacterial less likely without blood or severe systemic symptoms)  
- Food poisoning (no clear exposure)  
- Other causes of acute diarrhea (less likely given history)

---

## P - Plan

**Acute gastroenteritis:**  
- Supportive care focusing on hydration: Encourage oral rehydration with fluids including solutions like Dioralyte (oral rehydration salts) to replenish electrolytes.  
- Symptomatic treatment: Paracetamol for fever or malaise if needed.  
- Advise continued sick leave until diarrhea-free for 24-48 hours before returning to work.  
- Patient education on disease course: Usually self-limited over 3-5 days.  
- Monitor symptoms: Return if symptoms persist beyond one week, worsen, or if new symptoms such as blood in stools, severe abdominal pain, or persistent vomiting develop.  
- Follow-up: Re-assess if symptoms do not improve within the next week.
