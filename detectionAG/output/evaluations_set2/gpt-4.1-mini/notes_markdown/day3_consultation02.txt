## Assessment

1. **Severe headache and fever with generalized blanching rash in returning traveler**  
   This presentation raises concern for viral illnesses associated with mosquito bites, including dengue fever. The blanching rash and absence of bleeding or neurological signs lessen, but do not exclude, meningitis. Differential diagnosis includes:  
   - Dengue fever or other arboviral infections  
   - Viral exanthems  
   - Early meningitis (less likely without neck stiffness or photophobia)  
   - Other tropical infections (e.g., chikungunya, Zika)

---

## Plan

- **Urgent in-person clinical examination** at a medical hub today is strongly advised to enable a thorough physical assessment.  
- **Possible investigations** upon physical examination: blood tests for infectious causes (e.g., full blood count, dengue serology), and neurological examination.  
- **Monitoring:** Advice to seek emergency care if symptoms worsen (fever, rash changes such as non-blanching rash, neurological symptoms, or inability to tolerate fluids).  
- **Patient education:** Educated regarding the seriousness of symptoms and the need for urgent evaluation.  
- **Disposition:** Urgent outpatient assessment today; if unavailable, patient advised immediate hospital attendance.
