### Assessment  

1. **Suspected chest infection with possible pneumonia**  
   Acute onset breathlessness, pleuritic pain, fever, and hemoptysis raise suspicion for pneumonia or chest infection. The patient’s SLE and immunosuppressive therapy with methotrexate may increase infection risk.

2. **Rule out pulmonary embolism**  
   Although no history of recent travel or personal/family history of clots, acute pleuritic chest pain and hemoptysis warrant exclusion of pulmonary embolism.

3. **Anemia or bleeding etiology related to hemoptysis**  
   Coughing blood on two occasions is concerning and requires further evaluation.

4. **Concern about malignancy**  
   Low likelihood given sudden onset and symptom pattern, but lung cancer cannot be excluded without imaging especially with hemoptysis.
