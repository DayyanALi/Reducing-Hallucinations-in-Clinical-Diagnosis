## Subjective

**Chief Complaint:**  
Feeling generally unwell for 4 days with sore throat, runny nose, and now developing a cough.

**History of Present Illness:**  
The patient reports a 4-day history of illness that began with a sore throat and runny nose, now progressing to a dry cough starting yesterday. The sore throat was painful to swallow for 1–2 days but is improving. Nasal congestion is present with difficulty clearing the nose. The cough is dry, constant throughout the day, and not painful. Also noted are headaches (generalized, intermittent), muscle aches starting a few days ago, and general tiredness.

## Assessment

1. Acute viral upper respiratory infection (URI)  
   - Presents as sore throat, runny nose, dry cough, muscle aches, fatigue, night sweats, and resolving fever over 4 days.  
   - Differential includes early influenza and other viral respiratory illnesses.

2. Exertional dyspnea  
   - Mild shortness of breath with exertion, no chest pain or resting dyspnea reported; possibly related to acute illness or deconditioning.

---

## Plan

**Acute viral upper respiratory infection:**  
- Supportive care: rest, hydration, avoidance of strenuous activity  
- Continue paracetamol and ibuprofen as needed for fever and discomfort  
- Advise taking 1–2 days off work if possible  
- Monitor symptoms and return if no improvement after one week or sooner if worsening fever, chest pain, or increased shortness of breath  
- Patient education provided on symptom expectations and red flags  

**Exertional dyspnea:**  
- Monitor symptoms; likely transient and related to current viral illness  
- No immediate interventions; reassess if symptoms worsen or become persistent
