## A - Assessment

1. **Left-sided hearing loss with tinnitus and intermittent mild dizziness (6 weeks)**
    - Subacute, unilateral sensory changes suggest possible wax impaction or otologic etiology.
    - No evidence of infection, trauma, or acute vestibular syndrome.
    - Tinnitus possibly associated; differential includes otitis media (less likely without pain/discharge), cerumen impaction, sudden sensorineural hearing loss, Eustachian tube dysfunction, and inner ear pathology.
    - Mild dizziness may be vestibular, but true vertigo is denied.

---

## P - Plan

1. **Left-sided hearing loss/tinnitus/dizziness:**
   - Arrange in-person evaluation at clinic within 4–5 days for otoscopic examination to assess for wax impaction, infection, or other visible causes.  
   - Review for physical blockage (e.g., cerumen impaction) and further evaluation if findings are non-revealing.
   - Provide patient information leaflet on tinnitus (link to be added to patient's notes).
   - Advise patient to call support team to book face-to-face appointment; contact information to be provided in notes.
   - Educate patient on possible causes of tinnitus and the need for in-person ear exam.

   - Advised to contact clinic sooner if new symptoms arise or if condition worsens prior to appointment.
