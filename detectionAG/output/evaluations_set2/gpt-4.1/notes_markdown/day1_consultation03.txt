## A - Assessment

1. **Acute Unilateral Throbbing Headache with Aura (Suspected Migraine):**  
History and symptoms (unilateral throbbing pain, aura, photophobia, nausea, vomiting, exacerbation with movement, prior similar episode) are highly suggestive of migraine. No red flags for mass lesion, infection, or acute neurological pathology on current history. However, personal and family anxiety regarding brain tumor noted.

   - **Differential:** 
     - Migraine with aura (most likely)
     - Tension headache
     - Cluster headache
     - Intracranial mass (cancer/tumor, low suspicion given lack of focal neurology)
     - Meningitis/encephalitis (unlikely, no fever/rash/neck stiffness)
     - Sinusitis (less likely, no congestion or facial pain)
     - Medication or hormonal headache

2. **Health Anxiety (secondary to symptoms/family history):**  
Significant patient distress about possibility of brain cancer, influenced by friend’s family history.

   - **Differential:**
     - Health anxiety/disorder
     - Reasonable concern given symptom severity
