## Assessment

1. **Acute Gastroenteritis**  
   - Three-day history of watery diarrhea, left lower abdominal cramping, initial fever, malaise, and early vomiting; temporal association with recent restaurant meal. No blood in stool or vomit. Mildly affected family member with vomiting. Viral etiology most likely, but bacterial infection possible.
   - Differential: viral gastroenteritis, bacterial gastroenteritis (foodborne), less likely parasitic infection, inflammatory bowel disease flare (unlikely without history), medication effect (not suggested by history).
