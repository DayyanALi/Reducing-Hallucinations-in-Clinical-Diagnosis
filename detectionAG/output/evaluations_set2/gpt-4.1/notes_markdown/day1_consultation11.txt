## A – Assessment

1. **Acute Gastroenteritis (likely viral or foodborne bacterial):**  
   - 3 days of profuse, watery diarrhea, crampy abdominal pain, mild nausea, rare vomiting, subjective fever, no blood in stool. Similar illness in brother, suggesting an infectious etiology. Has kept up with fluids but describes ongoing significant losses and reduced urination, raising concern for potential dehydration.
   - Differential diagnosis: Infectious gastroenteritis (viral or bacterial), food poisoning, less likely inflammatory bowel disease (no prior history, but family history of colon cancer).

2. **Risk of Dehydration:**  
   - High stool frequency and ongoing losses, feelings of persistent thirst and decreased urinary frequency, subjective fevers.
   - Differential diagnosis: Volume depletion secondary to ongoing gastrointestinal losses.
