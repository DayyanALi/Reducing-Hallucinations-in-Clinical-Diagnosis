## A - Assessment

1. **Acute shortness of breath, 4 weeks postpartum (post-C-section); risk of venous thromboembolism (VTE)**
   - Sudden onset shortness of breath in a postpartum patient is concerning for pulmonary embolism (PE) or other VTE. Associated cough and subjective tachycardia increase concern. Chest pain only with deep inspiration. No leg symptoms reported.
   - Differential includes: pulmonary embolism, postpartum pneumonia/bronchitis, anxiety/panic, less likely postpartum cardiomyopathy, musculoskeletal pain post-C-section.

2. **Recent C-section (4 weeks ago)**
   - Recent major surgery increases VTE risk.
   
3. **Anxiety/palpitations**
   - Patient reports subjective stress and awareness of rapid heartbeat, may be reactive to symptoms.
