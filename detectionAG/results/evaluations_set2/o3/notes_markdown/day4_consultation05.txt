## P – Plan

### 1. Acute gastroenteritis

• Encourage oral rehydration: water and electrolyte solutions; aim ≥2 L/day.  
• Dietary advice: small, bland meals as tolerated; avoid dairy, caffeine, alcohol.  
• Red-flag education: seek in-person care/ED if severe abdominal pain, high fever (>38 °C), persistent vomiting preventing fluids, blood in stool or vomit, signs of dehydration (dizziness, reduced urine), or symptoms >48–72 h without improvement.
