## A - Assessment

1. **Gastroenteritis**  
   - Likely viral gastroenteritis based on recent history of diarrhea, abdominal cramps, and prior vomiting. Recent consumption of Chinese takeaway may be a contributing factor.

## P - Plan

1. **Gastroenteritis**  
   - Conservative management with oral rehydration solutions such as Dioralyte to maintain hydration and electrolyte balance.
   - Consider use of paracetamol (acetaminophen) if febrile or for symptomatic relief: 500 mg to 1000 mg orally, up to four times daily as needed.
   - Advise taking two to three days off work to rest and recuperate.
   - Monitor symptoms; if unresolved in three to four days, follow-up is necessary for further evaluation, potentially including stool sample testing.

**Follow-up**:
