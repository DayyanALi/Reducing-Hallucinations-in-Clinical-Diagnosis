## Assessment

1. **Right knee pain with mechanical symptoms (clicking), likely related to overuse/injury:**  
   Symptoms began after increasing running distance; mild transient swelling; no signs of acute injury like locking or instability. Differential includes overuse tendinopathy, meniscal irritation, early chondral injury, or ligament involvement (e.g., possible ACL strain). Neurologic symptoms (tingling toes) are intermittent and unexplained but may be related to mechanical irritation or transient nerve compression.

2. **Occasional tingling in right toes:**
