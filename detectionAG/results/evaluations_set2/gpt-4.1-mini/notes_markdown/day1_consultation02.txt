## Plan

- Prescribe a stronger topical steroid cream for application to affected areas.  
- Recommend use of emollients regularly, including in bath and shower, for at least 7 to 10 days.  
- Start oral antihistamine (e.g., fexofenadine) to address pruritus; stronger than previous over-the-counter option.  
- Advise patient to keep a diary of possible triggers (e.g., soaps, detergents, clothing, environmental exposures).  
- Educate patient on proper skin care routine and avoidance of irritants.  
- Instruct patient to return for follow-up in one week if symptoms do not improve or worsen.
