## Plan

**For acute gastroenteritis and dehydration risk:**  
- Encourage oral rehydration therapy with fluid replacement solutions such as Diarolyte (available over the counter).  
- Maintain hydration with clear fluids initially; soft diet with soups and mashed foods to be introduced as tolerated.  
- Symptomatic treatment with Paracetamol for fever and abdominal pain.  
- Avoid alcohol during illness.  
- Monitor symptoms; if diarrhea persists more than 48 hours, symptoms worsen, or signs of dehydration develop, patient to recontact for further evaluation.  
- Educate patient on signs of dehydration and when to seek urgent care.

**Follow-up:**  
- Patient to call if symptoms not improving in 1-2 days or if worsening.

**Patient instructions:**  
- Avoid milk products which appear to trigger vomiting.  
- Maintain hydration, use oral rehydration salts.  
- Use Paracetamol as needed for fever and pain.  
- Avoid alcohol.  
- Gradually resume normal diet as tolerated.

**Disposition:**  
Remain at home with close self-monitoring unless symptoms deteriorate.
