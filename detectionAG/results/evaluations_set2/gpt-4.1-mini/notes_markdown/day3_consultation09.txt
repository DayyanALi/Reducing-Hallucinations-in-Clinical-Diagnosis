## Assessment

1. **Left arm numbness, tingling, and weakness with focal finger involvement**  
   Symptoms progressive over two weeks, involving front side of second to fourth fingers, suggesting possible peripheral or central neurological etiology.

2. **Transient left eye peripheral vision loss (20 minutes)**  
   Suggestive of a transient neurological event; may be related to demyelinating disease or other neurological pathology.

3. **Recent viral illness**  
   Potentially contributing or preceding neurological symptoms.

**Differential diagnosis:**  
- Multiple sclerosis (given combination of neurological symptoms including visual disturbance and limb sensory/motor symptoms).  
- Peripheral neuropathy or nerve entrapment (e.g., ulnar neuropathy).  
- Cervical radiculopathy (though no neck pain).  
- Post-viral neurological syndrome.  
- Less likely, cerebrovascular event given transient vision loss but no other stroke signs.

---

## Plan

**For neurological symptoms:**  
- Arrange urgent neurology referral for detailed assessment.  
- Neurology may request brain imaging (MRI) and further investigations to rule out multiple sclerosis or other neurological causes.  
- No medications started pending diagnosis. Steroids may be considered if diagnosis confirmed.  
- Advise patient to seek emergency care (A&E) if symptoms worsen—progression of weakness to legs, worsening vision, severe headache, vomiting, seizures, or consciousness changes.

**For symptomatic management:**  
- Continue ibuprofen for muscle soreness as needed.  
- Continue vitamin D and melatonin as per patient’s regimen.  
- Encourage adherence to iron supplements if tolerable.

**Patient education:**  
- Explained possible diagnoses including multiple sclerosis, emphasizing early diagnosis and treatment opportunities.  
- Discussed importance of monitoring symptoms and urgent care if worsening occurs.  
- Reassured regarding the referral and next steps.

**Follow-up:**  
- Neurology consultation follow-up to be arranged urgently.  
- Primary care follow-up for medication adherence and symptom monitoring.

**Disposition:**  
- Outpatient management pending neurology evaluation.
