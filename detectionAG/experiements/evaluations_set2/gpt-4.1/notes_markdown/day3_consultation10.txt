## P: Plan

1. **Acute diarrhea**
    - **Dietary advice:**  
      - Continue soft diet (easily digestible foods such as crackers, toast, jam, boiled vegetables, mashed foods); avoid dairy, eggs, fish, and meat until diarrhea resolves for at least 48 hours.
      - Once symptom-free for 48 hours, gradual reintroduction of meat, eggs, and fish.
      - Yoghurt with probiotics encouraged.
    - **Hydration:**  
      - Maintain adequate oral fluid intake.
      - Oral rehydration solutions (e.g., Dioralyte) recommended if increased stool frequency/volume.
    - **Red flags/patient education:**  
      - Return or seek medical attention if diarrhea persists beyond one week, if stool becomes bloody, or if stool frequency increases to 6–8 times per day (due to risk of dehydration or possible bacterial etiology needing further workup).
      - If red flags develop, stool sample may be warranted for microbiological analysis and possible antibiotics.
    - **Medication:**  
      - No antidiarrheal or other medications initiated at this time.
    - **Monitoring:**  
      - Self-monitor symptoms including frequency and character of stools, hydration status.
    - **Follow-up:**  
      - Advised to review/app if ongoing concerns or deterioration.
    - **Disposition:**  
      - Outpatient self-management for current symptoms.
