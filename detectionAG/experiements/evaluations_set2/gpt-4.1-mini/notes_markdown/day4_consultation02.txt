## Assessment

**1. Severe headaches with nausea and reduced appetite:**  
Intermittent headaches localized to the front of the head associated with nausea and work-related stress. Differential includes tension headaches, migraine, medication side effects, or thyroid-related causes.

**2. Bilateral hand tingling:**  
New onset tingling in both hands without leg involvement. Differential includes peripheral neuropathy, carpal tunnel syndrome, metabolic causes including thyroid dysfunction, or stress-related symptoms.

**3. Palpitations and shortness of breath at rest:**  
Episodes of rapid heartbeat and shortness of breath occurring at rest; concerning for possible cardiac or thyroid-related etiology. Hyperthyroidism may contribute.

**4. Unintentional weight loss and decreased libido:**  
Weight loss without intentional dieting and loss of libido temporally associated with job change and symptoms; requires further evaluation.

**5. Hyperthyroidism, on treatment:**
