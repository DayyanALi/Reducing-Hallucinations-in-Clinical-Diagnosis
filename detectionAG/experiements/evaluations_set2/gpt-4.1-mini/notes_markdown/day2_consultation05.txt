## Assessment

1. **Transient Ischemic Attacks (TIAs) / Mini-strokes**  
   Young patient with episodic right-sided neurological symptoms (numbness, weakness, gait disturbance, impaired hand function) with increasing frequency. Family history of stroke and carotid artery disease. Working diagnosis is TIAs requiring urgent evaluation.

2. **Rule out other neurological causes**  
   Differential includes demyelinating disease, peripheral neuropathy, or other neurological disorders, but TIA favored given symptom pattern and risk factors.
