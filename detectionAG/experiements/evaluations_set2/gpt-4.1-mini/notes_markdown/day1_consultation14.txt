## P - Plan

**Acute viral illness:**  
- Supportive care: Advise rest, hydration, and symptomatic treatment with regular Paracetamol as needed for discomfort or fever.  
- Monitor symptoms: Patient instructed to return if cough persists beyond 7-10 days, worsens, or if fever recurs or breathing worsens.  
- Consider antibiotics only if secondary bacterial infection signs develop (e.g., persistent fever, productive cough with purulent sputum, worsening respiratory status).

**Chronic conditions (diabetes and hypertension):**  
- Continue current medications (Metformin and Lisinopril).  
- Encourage adherence and monitor glucose and blood pressure regularly.  
- Lifestyle counsel: Discussed reducing alcohol intake, ideally from 3 evenings/week to 2, then possibly less, to aid BP and diabetes control.

**Work and social advice:**  
- Recommend 1-2 days off work for rest due to cough and viral illness; gradual return as tolerated.  
- Advise avoiding close contact where possible while symptomatic.

**Follow-up:**  
- Return visit in 2-3 days if no improvement or sooner if respiratory symptoms worsen (e.g., worsening cough, breathlessness, chest pain, or if fever persists).  
- Consider evaluation for antibiotics if clinical signs suggest bacterial chest infection on reassessment.
