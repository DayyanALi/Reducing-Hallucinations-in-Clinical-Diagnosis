## Assessment

1. **Chest discomfort with ongoing symptoms suggestive of acute coronary syndrome (ACS)**  
   Presentation consistent with anginal chest pressure radiating to left arm and shoulder, accompanied by nausea, diaphoresis, and dyspnea. High suspicion for myocardial ischemia or infarction given symptom characteristics and family history. Differential includes unstable angina, myocardial infarction, possibly aortic dissection (less likely), or other non-cardiac chest pain.

2. **Hypertension**  
   Known condition; contributes to cardiovascular risk.

3. **Tobacco use disorder**  
   Significant smoking history contributing to cardiovascular risk.
